Couscous

كسكسي

Steamed over aromatic broth in a traditional couscoussier, adorned with tender roasted vegetables, chickpeas, and a fiery dollop of harissa.

Brik à l'Oeuf

بريك

Whisper-thin pastry sheets fried crisp to order, cradling a runny whole egg, fresh parsley, capers, and tuna dusted with cumin.

📍 Bab Souika, Tunis STREET FOOD

Lablabi

لبلابي

A soul-warming peasant bowl of slow-simmered chickpeas poured over torn stale crusty bread, laced with cumin, virgin olive oil, soft egg, and harissa.

Harissa

A few words of Tunisian

Essential Darija phrases to order, compliment the chef, and share warmth across every table.

01
Aslema عسلامة
Hello / Welcome
02
Yaishek يعيشك
Thank you
03
Bqaddech? بقداش؟
How much is it?
04
Behi باهي
Okay / Good
05
Beslema بسلامة
Goodbye
06
Bnina barcha بنينة برشة
Very delicious!
07
Bel-harr? بالحار؟
Is it spicy?
08
Chwaya barka شوية بركة
Just a little bit
09
L'hssab, yaishek الحساب يعيشك
The bill, please
10
Sahha! صحة!
Bon appétit / To your health
